Partnership Models

Two ways to work with us

We work with brands at every stage — from local producers looking to scale, to established names seeking a reliable route to market across Tanzania's Northern Circuit.

Distribution Partner
We Distribute Your Brand

You focus on production. We handle the rest. As your distribution partner, your products are placed directly into hotels, lodges, safari camps, restaurants and supermarkets across Arusha and the Northern Circuit — through relationships built over two decades.

Sole Distribution
Exclusive Representation in the Northern Circuit

For brands seeking a committed, exclusive presence in this market, we offer sole distribution agreements within the Northern Circuit. Your brand receives dedicated sales effort, warehousing and a team fully invested in your growth — with the credibility of an established local name behind it.

What We Distribute

From household names to local rising brands

We distribute across every major category — and we work with both internationally recognised names and local brands ready to grow. If the product is right, the size of the brand does not matter.

HOW IT WORKS

From Enquiry to Shelves in Four Steps

1
Submit an Enquiry

Fill in the partnership form below with your brand, product range and what you are looking to achieve. The more detail the better.

2

A member of our team gets in touch within 48 hours. We discuss the opportunity, understand your product and explore whether there is a strong fit.
